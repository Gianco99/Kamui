# A DV whose fit covariance has a negative eigenvalue can get NaN uncertainties, and the vertex selector then drops it silently.

- **JMTucker:** `MFVNeutralino/plugins/VertexSelector.cc:384-443`. `use_vertex` returns one long conjunction of comparisons, and every comparison with a NaN is false, including the ones set to cut nothing. `mfvVerticesAux` (`MFVNeutralino/python/Vertexer_cff.py:17`) runs this selector in the ntuple job, so such a DV never reaches the ntuple.
- **Kamui:** `vertexing/plugins/DVProducer.cc:150-167, 188-189` (`jmtErrorsFinite` and its use in `selectionQuantities`). Reproduced on purpose to match JMTucker: `DV_dBVErr` is NaN when any uncertainty the JMTucker selector compares is NaN (the errors on the DV's distance from the origin in 2D and 3D, on its distance to the beamspot, and on its miss distance to the PV along the DV's momentum), so the DV fails `tightDV`. A covariance with a negative eigenvalue often gives finite values for all of them, and then both frameworks keep the DV.
- **Impact:** Minor: 552 of 518,286 DVs in the Kamui validation outputs have a NaN `DV_dBVErr`, almost all outside the beampipe, and only 10 of them, all in MC, pass every other tight cut. The drop removes about 3e-5 of tight DVs and none in data.
- **Fix:** Decide deliberately: repair the covariance before computing uncertainties, or drop these DVs with an explicit cut that shows up in the cutflow.
